Account Protection

Your Security is Our Priority

We use multiple layers of protection to help keep your account and funds secure.

🔐

Two-Factor Authentication

Add an extra layer of security to your account with 2FA. Even if someone knows your password, they still need your second factor to sign in.

🛡️

Encrypted Connections

All data transmitted between your device and our servers is protected with industry-standard HTTPS encryption.

🔑

Secure Authentication

Passwords are securely hashed and never stored in plain text. We follow modern authentication best practices.

✓

Withdrawal Verification

Withdrawal requests go through additional security checks before processing to help protect your funds.

📊

Transaction Monitoring

We monitor account activity for unusual patterns so we can respond quickly to potential security concerns.

🔔

Security Notifications

Receive alerts for important account events such as logins, password changes, and withdrawal requests.

⚠️

Important Security Notice

BYWEALTH INVEST will never ask you to share your password, private keys, or recovery phrase.

If anyone contacting you claims to be from BYWEALTH INVEST and requests this information, do not provide it. Report the incident to our support team immediately.

Recommended Security Practices

1

Enable Two-Factor Authentication

Turn on 2FA in your account settings for stronger protection.

2

Use a Strong, Unique Password

Choose a long password that you don’t use on other websites.

3

Keep Your Contact Details Updated

Ensure your email and phone number are current so you receive security alerts.

4

Be Cautious with Links and Messages

Avoid clicking suspicious links. Always access the platform directly through the official website.

5

Review Account Activity Regularly

Check your transaction history and login activity from time to time.
