Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide when creating an account or using the platform, including:

  • Full name, email address, phone number and country
  • Account activity (deposits, withdrawals, investments)
  • Transaction IDs and wallet addresses you submit
  • Technical data such as IP address, browser type and device information

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account
  • To process deposits, withdrawals and investments
  • To communicate important account and security notices

  • To improve the platform and user experience

3. Data Protection

We use industry-standard security measures including HTTPS encryption and secure password hashing. We do not store cryptocurrency private keys. Payment and blockchain interactions are handled through verified processes.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal data. We may share information only with service providers necessary to operate the platform (e.g. email delivery, security monitoring) or when required by law.

5. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of your personal data. Contact us at support@bywealthinvest.com to exercise these rights.

6. Cookies

We use essential cookies for authentication, session management and security. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

7. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available on this page.

8. Contact

For privacy-related questions, email support@bywealthinvest.com.
