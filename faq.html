Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers to common questions about the platform.

Click “Get Started” or “Create Account”, fill in your full name, email, phone, country and password, then accept the Terms, Privacy Policy and Risk Disclosure. You will receive an email verification link.
Go to the Deposit page, select the cryptocurrency and network, enter the amount, copy the displayed wallet address (or scan the QR code), send the funds from your personal wallet, then paste the transaction ID (TXID) and submit. The deposit remains Pending until verified.
Supported assets are configurable by the administrator. Common examples include Bitcoin (BTC), Ethereum (ETH), USDT and USDC. Always check the Deposit page for the currently enabled options and networks.
After the blockchain confirms the transaction and our team verifies the TXID, the balance is credited. Timing depends on network congestion and verification workload. You will be notified when the status changes from Pending.
Submit a withdrawal request with the cryptocurrency, network, amount and destination wallet address. The request goes through security review (Pending → Under Review → Approved → Processing → Completed). Additional verification may be required.
Cryptocurrency deposits are free (you only pay the blockchain network fee). Withdrawals carry a platform fee plus network fee. Investment products show their management fee on the product page. See the Pricing page for full details.
